# WilhelmSK

WilhelmSK is a marine instrument display app for **iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, tvOS, and CarPlay**. It connects to a [SignalK](https://signalk.org) server on your vessel's network and renders live data as customizable analog and digital gauges — wind, speed, heading, depth, engine, tanks, electrical, autopilot, AIS, and any custom SignalK path.

Discovery is automatic via Bonjour. Manual host/port entry is also supported.

---

## Get the App

Search **WilhelmSK** on the App Store. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. The Apple Watch app installs automatically from your iPhone.
